How it works

Departure time
Use the departure airport’s local time. Daylight saving time is included.
Left and right
Sides are relative to the direction of travel. Sunrise and sunset preferences use the sun’s direction at that event.
Sun percentages
Percentages show the share of the flight with the sun in each direction. Shade favors less exposure; sunshine favors more exposure.
Sunrise and sunset
Events are horizon crossings along the moving flight path at an estimated altitude. Times use the departure airport’s time zone. Flights without an event say so.
Accuracy
Estimates use a great-circle route and SunCalc solar positions. Actual routing, weather, aircraft attitude and window geometry are not included.
